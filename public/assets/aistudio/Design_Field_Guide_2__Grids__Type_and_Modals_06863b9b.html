Rules of thumb

  • Never truncate a status or a date. Let it wrap, or restructure the card.
  • 14px is the floor for UI text. Phones in sunlight punish 11–12px.
  • Two weights are enough: 400 for text, 600 for emphasis. Size can handle the rest.
  • Say the unit once. “Daily rate” as the label, “65,000 RWF” as the value.
  • Use grey carefully. #767676 on white is about the lightest grey that still passes contrast.
  • Write dates in full: “12 Mar 2026”, not “2026-03-12”, for people who aren’t programmers.

Six modal designs, all live

Press each button. Close with the button, by clicking outside, or with the Esc key. They use the browser’s built-in dialog element, which handles focus and keyboard correctly.

1. Centered dialog

For short decisions with one or two choices. The default for desktop.

2. Bottom sheet

Best on phones. Thumb-reachable, easy to dismiss.

3. Side drawer

For details or edit forms while the list stays visible behind.

4. Full-screen form

For long forms like a new booking. Avoids a cramped box on phones.

5. Danger confirmation

For irreversible actions. Asks the user to type the plate.

6. No modal at all

Inline confirm. Often better than interrupting.

What makes a good modal

Do

  • Title says the action: “Send repair request?”, not “Are you sure?”.
  • Buttons repeat the verb: “Send request”, “Delete car”, not “OK” or “Yes”.
  • Put the main action on the right, cancel on the left.
  • Close on Esc and on outside click, except forms with unsaved data.
  • Match the size to the job: small for choices, drawer for edits, full screen for long forms.
  • Dim the page behind, enough to separate it, not to black.

Don’t

  • Stack a modal on top of another modal.
  • Use a modal for something the user can undo. Show a toast with “Undo” instead.
  • Put long, scrolling content in a small centered box.
  • Hide the close button, or make it tiny.
  • Add animation beyond a quick 150ms fade.
  • Make every dialog look the same when the stakes differ. Danger gets a different look.

Practice

  1. Rebuild the car card from your app using the “after” layout. Show all dates in full.
  2. Swap your stat cards for grid A or C. Count how many borders you removed.
  3. Replace one “Are you sure?” dialog with an Undo toast, and keep the danger dialog for deletes only.

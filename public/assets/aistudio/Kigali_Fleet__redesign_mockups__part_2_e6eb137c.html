Kigali Fleet, redesigned: part 2

Seven more screens. Each one is built around what that screen is really for: a repair ledger, a handover form, a phone roster, a checklist, a price table, a settings form, and the public site your guests see. Same rules as before: flat, hairlines, no cut-off words. Scroll sideways on a phone.

A repair is a job card with a garage, dates and a bill. So it is a table, with costs right-aligned and totalled. “Release car” is the only filled button, and only on the car still in the garage.
